## 1️⃣ Perfect Wudu Challenge 💧

### Game Overview:
Learn the correct steps of Wudu (ablution) through interactive gameplay!

### 🎯 Game Modes:

**Level 1-10: Drag & Drop** 🎯
- Arrange wudu steps in correct order
- Drag steps from bottom to drop zones
- All steps must be in perfect sequence
- Visual feedback on correct/wrong placement

**Level 11-20: Quick Tap** ⚡
- Tap steps in the correct order
- Speed challenge mode
- Instant feedback on mistakes
- Progressive difficulty

### 📊 Difficulty Progression:
- Level 1-5: 4 basic steps
- Level 6-10: 6 detailed steps
- Level 11-15: 8 comprehensive steps
- Level 16-20: 10 complete steps

### 🌟 Points System:
- **30 points**: Perfect (0 mistakes, <30 seconds)
- **20 points**: Great (1-2 mistakes)
- **15 points**: Good (3+ mistakes)

### 📚 Educational Content:
All 10 Wudu steps included:
1. نِيَّة - Intention (Niyyah)
2. بِسْمِ اللَّهِ - Say Bismillah
3. غَسْلُ الْكَفَّيْنِ - Wash both hands
4. الْمَضْمَضَةُ - Rinse mouth
5. الْاِسْتِنْشَاقُ - Sniff water into nose
6. غَسْلُ الْوَجْهِ - Wash face
7. غَسْلُ الْيَدَيْنِ - Wash arms to elbows
8. مَسْحُ الرَّأْسِ - Wipe head
9. مَسْحُ الْأُذُنَيْنِ - Wipe ears
10. غَسْلُ الْقَدَمَيْنِ - Wash feet

### 🎨 Features:
- Beautiful Islamic-themed UI
- Blue gradient background
- Sound effects for correct/wrong
- Timer and mistake counter
- Progress tracking
- Level unlock system

## 2️⃣ 99 Names of Allah Puzzle 🕌

### Game Overview:
Learn and memorize all 99 Beautiful Names of Allah through 3 different game modes!

### 🎯 Game Modes:

**Level 1-33: Word Scramble** 🔤
- Letters of the name are scrambled
- Unscramble to form correct transliteration
- Meaning shown as clue
- Hint available after 3 mistakes

**Level 34-66: Memory Match** 🧩
- Match Arabic name with its meaning
- Match transliteration with Urdu
- 8 cards grid (4 pairs of current name + 4 distractors)
- Find all 4 cards of current name

**Level 67-99: Type Challenge** ⌨️
- Arabic name and meaning shown
- Type the correct transliteration
- Auto-complete suggestions
- Hint after 2 mistakes

### 🌟 Points System:
- **40 points**: Perfect (0 mistakes, <20 seconds)
- **30 points**: Great (1-2 mistakes, <40 seconds)
- **20 points**: Good (3+ mistakes)

### 📚 Complete Data:
All 99 Names included with:
- ✅ Arabic text (الرَّحْمَـٰنُ)
- ✅ Transliteration (Ar-Rahman)
- ✅ English meaning (The Most Merciful)
- ✅ Urdu translation (بہت مہربان)

### 🎨 Features:
- Amber/Orange gradient (Islamic gold theme)
- Color-coded by difficulty:
  - Purple: Scramble mode (1-33)
  - Orange: Match mode (34-66)
  - Teal: Type mode (67-99)
- Progress tracking (X/99 names learned)
- Sound effects
- Beautiful card animations
- Level completion badges

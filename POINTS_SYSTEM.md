# 🎯 Noor App - Complete Points System Guide

## Kaise Points Milte Hain? (How to Earn Points?)

### 📖 Quran & Reading (قرآن)
- **10 points** - Complete any Surah
- Location: Quran Reader Screen
- Action: Surah complete karne ke baad top-right "✓" button press karein

### 🤲 Prayer & Worship (نماز)
- **5 points** - Log each prayer (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha)
- **25 points daily max** - All 5 prayers logged
- Location: Prayer Times Screen
- Action: Har prayer ke saamne "+" button dabayein

### 📚 Learning Lessons (تعلیم)
- **20 points per lesson** - Complete any learning lesson
- Lessons include:
  - ✅ Shahada (Declaration of Faith)
  - ✅ Wudu Guide (Ablution)
  - ✅ Salah Guide (Prayer)
  - ✅ Six Pillars of Iman
  - ✅ Islamic Etiquettes
  - ✅ Halal & Haram
  - ✅ Arabic Alphabet
- Location: Learning Hub
- Action: "Mark as Learned" button press karein

### 🤲 Duas & Supplications (دعائیں)
- **15 points** - Learn a Dua
- **5 points** - Complete Daily Adhkar
- Location: Dua Screen / Daily Adhkar
- Action: Tap on any adhkar to mark complete

### 📿 Tasbih Counter (تسبیح)
- **5 points** - Complete 33 count
- **10 points** - Complete 99 or 100 count
- **25 points** - Complete 500 count
- **50 points** - Complete 1000 count
- Location: Tasbih Screen
- Action: Reach target count automatically

### 📖 Hadith Reading (حدیث)
- **10 points** - Read any Hadith
- Location: Hadith Screen
- Action: Tap on any hadith to read & earn points

### 📚 Story Reading (کہانیاں)
- **15 points** - Complete Islamic story
- Location: Stories Section
- Action: Read story and mark as complete

### 🎮 Games (کھیل)
**Memory Game:**
- **30 points** - Complete with 3 stars
- **20 points** - Complete with 2 stars
- **10 points** - Complete with 1 star

**Prayer Quiz:**
- **25 points** - Perfect score (5/5)
- **15 points** - Good score (3-4/5)
- **5 points** - Participation (1-2/5)

Location: Kids Zone
Action: Play games and complete levels
